To fetch the employee name and the department name they belong to, assuming there's a relationship between the Employee and Department tables via a foreign key like department_id, here’s the SQL query:

SELECT e.employee_name, d.department_name
FROM Employee e
JOIN Department d ON e.department_id = d.department_id;
